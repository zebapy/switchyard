Accent toning plan — ember

The accent (--color-ember #ffae5c / #ff9438) reads as "everywhere." Cause: it's used for selection, primary actions, and nearly every hover. When every hover glows orange, the accent stops meaning anything.

1The principle

Accent should mark exactly two things: (1) the one selected/active item, and (2) the one primary action on a surface. Everything else — hover, focus elevation, secondary icons, status — uses neutrals or its own semantic color.

Rule of thumb: if more than one ember thing is visible in a region at rest or on hover, it's too much.

2What changes, by category

tone down
Hover affordances — the main offender
Icon buttons, menu items, chips, cards all flip to ember on hover (hover:text-ember, hover:bg-ember-tint, hover:border-ember-edge, group-hover:text-ember). This is ~half of all usage and the reason it feels everywhere.
hover:text-ember→hover:text-ink hover:bg-ember-tint→hover:bg-hover hover:border-ember-edge→hover:border-guide
